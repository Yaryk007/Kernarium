# Golden frames of the thermal wire (vsock 5559), the contract between the
# guest's thermal HAL (android/lineage-24/overlay/thermal) and the host
# (Andarium/AndariumCore ThermalProtocol.swift).
#
# One frame per line: `name: <hex bytes>`. A line that starts with `#` is a
# comment, and the comment above a frame says what it means.
#
# A record is 8 bytes, host to guest only: "ATH1" (41 54 48 31), one byte with
# the Android severity the host wants reported (0 NONE, 1 LIGHT, 2 MODERATE,
# 3 SEVERE, 4 CRITICAL) and three reserved bytes that are zero. Android's
# EMERGENCY (5) and SHUTDOWN (6) are not severities of the wire.
#
# The host's encoder writes every frame that is not a `reject-` frame, and the
# guest reads each of them as the severity described; the application's policy
# sends NONE, SEVERE and CRITICAL, and MODERATE while Low Power Mode holds a
# 120 Hz display to 60, never LIGHT. The guest closes the connection on every
# `reject-` frame and keeps the severity it had; the host never encodes one.

# NONE: the host's thermal state is nominal or fair, and neither serious nor
# critical was the state in the last 120 s.
none: 41 54 48 31 00 00 00 00

# LIGHT: the guest reports it; the application does not send it.
light: 41 54 48 31 01 00 00 00

# MODERATE: the display is the 120 Hz one and iOS is in Low Power Mode, which holds
# the screen to 60; the guest renders no more than 60. A host that is also hot
# sends the hotter severity.
moderate: 41 54 48 31 02 00 00 00

# SEVERE: serious is the hottest of the host's thermal states now and in the
# last 120 s (the hold after a state ended), so critical is neither the state
# nor held; the guest caps the frame rate.
severe: 41 54 48 31 03 00 00 00

# CRITICAL: the host's thermal state is critical now or was in the last 120 s
# (the hold after critical ended); the guest caps the frame rate.
critical: 41 54 48 31 04 00 00 00

# Severity 5 is Android's EMERGENCY, which the guest never reports.
reject-emergency: 41 54 48 31 05 00 00 00

# Severity 6 is Android's SHUTDOWN, which the guest never reports.
reject-shutdown: 41 54 48 31 06 00 00 00

# The magic's last byte is 2, not 1: not this protocol's record.
reject-magic: 41 54 48 32 00 00 00 00

# A reserved byte is not zero.
reject-reserved: 41 54 48 31 01 00 01 00
