# Golden frames of the gamepad wire (vsock 5558), the contract between the
# guest's gamepad relay (android/lineage-24/overlay/gamepad) and the host
# (Andarium/AndariumCore GamepadProtocol.swift).
#
# One frame per line: `name: <hex bytes>`. A line that starts with `#` is a
# comment, and the comment above a frame says what it means. Every number is
# little endian.
#
# A frame is an 8-byte header and a payload: "AGP1" (41 47 50 31), a u16 type
# and a u16 payload length of at most 4400. Both ends close the connection on
# every `reject-` frame, and neither encodes one.

# DESTROY (2), host to guest: slot 1, three reserved bytes.
destroy-slot-1: 41 47 50 31 02 00 04 00 01 00 00 00

# INPUT (3), host to guest: slot 0, one reserved byte, length 3, the report
# 01 80 80 with its report ID.
input-slot-0: 41 47 50 31 03 00 07 00 00 00 03 00 01 80 80

# HELLO (16), guest to host: protocol version 1, 8 slots, one reserved byte.
hello: 41 47 50 31 10 00 04 00 01 00 08 00

# The magic's last byte is 2, not 1: not this protocol's frame.
reject-magic: 41 47 50 32 02 00 04 00 01 00 00 00

# Type 255 is no frame of the protocol.
reject-type: 41 47 50 31 ff 00 04 00 01 00 00 00

# A payload length of 4401 is over the limit; the header alone decides.
reject-length: 41 47 50 31 03 00 31 11
